The WRITE loop

WRITE staked in the backstop sets vault capacity. Capacity writes premium. Fees paid in WRITE are burned and bonded. Demand is written into the contracts, not into a marketing plan.

  1. Backstop$10M WRITE staked

    The safety module that backstops every vault.

  2. Vault capacity$40M

    Cap = k × backstop value. Depositors fill it with Stock Tokens.

  3. Premium written each week$120k

    Market makers pay it in USDG, up front, twice a week.

  4. WRITE burned and bonded$18k a week

    Fees at a discount in WRITE, half burned. $50k bonded per market maker.

Burn and bonds tighten supply, staking grows the backstop, and the loop starts again with more capacity.
